About the Tracker

  • Export New — downloads only patients not yet exported, then marks them as exported
  • Export All — downloads all patients without changing the tracker
  • Reset Tracker — clears the export history, marking all patients as pending
  • The export status is stored locally in your browser and persists across sessions
